🧪 Stage 5 · Automatic assignment demo

Independent classroom simulation. It does not assign real Firebase customers or reserve Firebase machines. Your shared queue above is unchanged.

Demo waiting line: Customer A → Customer B → Customer C. The first customer gets the next free washer. Each customer has five simulated minutes to check in.

To test expiry: press “Advance 1 minute” five times without checking in. The reservation expires and the next customer is assigned automatically. To test a successful booking, check in and then finish the cycle.
